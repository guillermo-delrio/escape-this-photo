# Sample rooms

Three rooms the pipeline built, shipped so a fresh clone's gallery works without a Meta key (the server copies this
folder to `local/data/` on first start). Each one was escaped by a blind playtester before it was published.

| Room | Photo | Licence of the photo |
|---|---|---|
| `84303e52a8` The Sunlit Bedroom | Monticello, master bedroom, looking southwest. Historic American Buildings Survey, Library of Congress (HABS VA,2-CHAR.V,1-35) | Public domain |
| `50dbd9e6c4` The Farm Bedroom | Bauernhofmuseum Jexhof 5, by Edelmauswaldgeist, Wikimedia Commons | CC0 |
| `a04c82250f` The Locked Study | Abbotsford House Study Room (49792969286), by Michael D Beckwith, Wikimedia Commons | CC0 |

`photo.jpg` and the crops taken from it come from these photos. The other walls (`wall-*.jpg`), close-ups
(`detail-*.jpg`), interiors (`interior-*.jpg`) and note paper (`note-*.jpg`) were painted by Meta's Muse Image from the
photo. The puzzles and words in `room.json` were written by Meta's Muse Spark, and the outlines are SAM 3.1's.
